> **Erratum:** O23 withdrawn at D3 (R1): see the outcome (`2026-10-28-p5-rdv-libre-outcome.md`).

> **For agentic workers:** this is a short plan with no code in it, on the owner's request (2026-10-28: « option 1 », the lighter
> method). The design reference is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-28-p5-rdv-libre-design.md` (the P5 scope dossier, cited

> its § 6 the Review Focus. Read the dossier sections your group names; never re-derive them.

**Goal:** Let a client book a free-time appointment (« RDV libre ») on a pro's public page: pick a slot computed from the pro's
hours, pay per slot, book for free, or spend a held ticket, with the same identity, payment and abuse rules as the P4 séances.

**Architecture:** P4's patterns, unchanged (D § 3.1): pure modules and `(db, …, now)` DB cores under `src/lib/*`, thin
`"use server"` and `server-only` wrappers pinned by source guards, identity from the slug and the fresh session only. New: the
pure slot generator `src/lib/availability.ts`, the slot loader `src/lib/slots.ts`, the public RDV reads and action, the page
`/[slug]/rdv/[ticketTypeId]` and its client component, the RDV cards on `/[slug]` (D § 3.2 module list, D § 3.3 data flow).
The engine `bookRdv` (`src/lib/booking/claims.ts`) and the EXCLUDE `occurrence_rdv_excl` already exist; P5 adds the slot grid
on top and the six additive engine changes of D § 3.15.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.2.9 App Router, React 19, Drizzle 0.45.2 + Neon HTTP, Better Auth 1.6.19, Stripe 22, zod 4.4.3,
@date-fns/tz, Tailwind v4, node:test via tsx.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-tickets-evenements-design.md` (binding, `L<n>`). Design and contract of this plan:
the dossier above (it doubles as the P5 contract: no separate contract file). Earlier outcomes: P2 `2026-10-12-p2-domaine-outcome.md`,
P3 `2026-10-14-p3-back-office-outcome.md`, P4 `2026-10-21-p4-public-outcome.md`.

## Review Focus

D § 6 is binding: identity from input, the slot grid and loader/engine drift, double booking and races, time zones and DST,
payments and holds, abuse bounds, tenancy, copy and accessibility. The five most likely to bite, checked first by Group D's
review:

1. A `held_ticket` spend or a resume URL reached by a typed address, a guest cookie or an impersonating session (T9).
2. An off-grid or sub-minute start accepted, or the loader offering a slot the engine refuses (T3, T6, T9).
3. Two bookings on one slot, or a lapsed hold still shown busy (T6, T9, the two-tab browser check of T12).
4. A slot built from a browser or host-zone `Date`, or a DST day offering a wrong or duplicate start (T3, T6, T11).
5. Another pro's séances, time off, RDVs or ids touching this pro's slots (second-pro cases of T6, T7 and T9).
